Privacy Policy

Pirith app (Android and iOS, lk.pitaka.pirith) and the website pitaka.lk/pirith · Last updated 23 November 2026

Pirith lets you listen to Buddhist Paritta chanting and read the Pali text with its meaning. It is offered free of charge as a gift of Dhamma. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

The apps

  • The apps need no account, sign-in or registration.
  • The apps do not collect or send any personal data. They contain no advertising, no analytics and no tracking libraries.
  • All chants and texts are inside the app, so it works without an internet connection.
  • Your playlists, settings (language, dark mode, text size, repeat counts) and play counts are kept only on your device. They are never sent to us and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions (Android)

  • Notifications and foreground media playback: to show the playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen, and to keep the chanting playing when the screen is off.
  • Internet: only to open links that you tap, such as the website. The app does not send any information about you.

The website

  • The website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics. Your playlists and settings are kept in your own browser's storage.
  • Like every web server, ours keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) for security and to keep the site running. These logs are not used to identify visitors and are not shared.

Children

The app and website are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: pathnirvana@gmail.com
