The water washing the seat of the one who recites this protective chant,

even that water utterly destroys all dangers;

and which brings about the safe delivery of the unborn child at that very instant;

taught by the Lord of the World to the Elder Angulimala;

we shall now recite that protective chant, which holds great power and lasts for an aeon.

Sister, since I was born into the Noble Birth,

I am not aware of having intentionally deprived any living being of life.

By this truth, may you be well, and may your unborn child be well.
