we recite that protection, endowed with the power of the virtues of the Arahant Sivali.

The Buddha named Padumuttara, who had the eye of wisdom into all things,
arose as the leader of the world a hundred thousand aeons ago.

The great elder Sivali was worthy of receiving the requisites.
He was dear to gods and humans, dear to the Brahmas, and supreme.
Dear to nagas and garudas, with contented faculties: to him I pay homage.

(a mantra passage)
By the power of him who is honoured by gods and humans, may all gains come to me!

For seven days I was lodged at the door of the womb, afflicted with great suffering.
My mother, too, suffered greatly like this, because of the consent she once gave.

While my hair was being shaved, I attained arahantship.
Gods, nagas and humans bring the requisites to me.

The leaders named Padumuttara and Vipassi
I honoured joyfully, especially with the requisites.

Therefore, through the distinction of those deeds, abundant and supreme
gain I receive everywhere: in forest, in village, on water and on land.

Then, with requisites brought by the gods for my sake, the greatly wise,
the great hero, the leader of the world, together with the Sangha,

the Buddha, attended by me, went and saw Revata.
Then, having gone to Jetavana, he placed me in the foremost position.

When the leader went to see Revata,
the supreme leader of the world went with thirty thousand monks.

"Monks, among my disciples Sivali is foremost of those who receive gains":
so the Teacher, the friend of the whole world, praised me in the assemblies.

My defilements are burnt away; all existences are uprooted.
Like an elephant that has broken its bonds, I live without taints.

Welcome indeed was my coming to the presence of the supreme Buddha.
The three knowledges are attained; the Buddha's teaching is done.

The four analytical knowledges and the eight liberations,
the six higher knowledges are realised; the Buddha's teaching is done.

The great elder Sivali, a son of the Buddha, a disciple of the Victor,
a great hero of lofty power, by his power (made) the Victor's dispensation (shine).

Protecting by the power of his virtue, making us wealthy and renowned,
by such majestic power may Sivali protect us always!

The Buddha sat at the foot of the Bodhi tree; (that power) lasts for an aeon.
By him who crushed the army of Mara, may Sivali protect us always!

Having fulfilled the ten perfections, he went forth in the Victor's dispensation.
A son of Gotama, the Sakyan son: the elder Sivali is my (refuge).

Among the eighty great disciples, the elder Punna was the renowned one.
Among those foremost in receiving wealth and gains, Sivali is therefore supreme.

Thus the Buddhas are inconceivable; the Buddhas' teachings are inconceivable.
For those who have faith in the inconceivable, the result is inconceivable.

By their truth and virtue, by the power of their patience and loving-kindness,
may they protect me, destroying all suffering!

By their truth and virtue, by the power of their patience and loving-kindness,
may they protect me, destroying all fear!

By their truth and virtue, by the power of their patience and loving-kindness,
may they protect me, destroying all disease!
