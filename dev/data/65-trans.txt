Who stopped the fierce flood of samsara and reached Nibbana,
whose pure wisdom spreads wide, steadfast, like a plate of gold,
ever radiant in body, who taught the true Dhamma:
bowing low, I worship my fully Awakened One, the moon of majesty.

Which removes every doubt and so destroys all delusion,
which like armour guards one's life in the battle with Mara,
which gives joy to those who follow it and destroys the defilements:
with devotion I always worship the glorious true Dhamma, the jewel lamp.

Who give happiness and destroy all the evil that should be destroyed,
who shatter the fearsome bonds of existence and ever fulfil the virtues,
who give up the comforts they have and set their eyes on the pure far shore:
with delight I always worship the noble great Sangha, rich in merit.
