The Khandha Protection

Thus
by me
it was heard:
On one occasion
the Blessed One
in Savatthi
was dwelling
in Jeta's Grove,
Anathapindika's
monastery.

Now at that time
in Savatthi
a certain monk
was bitten by a snake
and passed away.

Then
many monks
where the Blessed One was,
there they approached.
Having approached,
the Blessed One,
having paid homage,
to one side
they sat down.

To one side
seated,
those monks
to the Blessed One
said this:

Venerable sir, here in Savatthi
a certain monk
was bitten by a snake
and has passed away.

Monks, surely that monk did not
to the four royal snake families
with a mind of loving-kindness
radiate.

For if
monks, that monk
to the four royal snake families
with a mind of loving-kindness
had radiated,
not
monks, would that monk
having been bitten by a snake
have passed away.

What are
the four royal snake families?
The Virupakkha royal snake family,
the Erapatha royal snake family,
the Chabyaputta royal snake family,
and the Kanhagotamaka royal snake family.

Monks, surely that monk did not
to these four royal snake families
with a mind of loving-kindness
radiate.

For if
monks, that monk
to these four royal snake families
with a mind of loving-kindness
had radiated,
not
monks, would that monk
having been bitten by a snake
have passed away.

I allow you,
monks,
to these four royal snake families
with a mind of loving-kindness
to radiate,
for your own safeguarding,
for your own protection,
for your own shielding.

The Blessed One said this.
Having said this,
the Well-Gone One,
then furthermore,
said this
the Teacher:

With the Virupakkhas
is my
loving-kindness.
Loving-kindness
is with the Erapathas
of mine.
With the Chabyaputtas
is my
loving-kindness.
Loving-kindness
is with the Kanhagotamakas too
of mine.

With the footless
is my
loving-kindness.
Loving-kindness
is with the two-footed
of mine.
With the four-footed
is my
loving-kindness.
Loving-kindness
is with the many-footed
of mine.

May the footless not harm me,
may the two-footed not harm me,
may the four-footed not harm me,
may the many-footed not harm me.

May all beings,
all living creatures,
and all creatures entirely,
all of them,
good fortune
see;
may no suffering come to anyone!

Infinite are the virtues of the Buddha.
Infinite are the virtues of the Dhamma.
Infinite are the virtues of the Sangha.

Finite are
creeping things:
snakes,
scorpions,
centipedes,
spiders,
lizards,
and mice.
A protection has been made by me.
A safeguard has been made by me.
Let the creatures retreat.
the creatures.
I myself
pay homage
to the Blessed One,
and pay homage
to the seven Fully Enlightened Buddhas.
