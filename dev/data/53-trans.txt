The Eight Stanzas of Joyous Victory

Creating a thousand hands, all equipped with weapons,
riding the elephant Girimekhala, accompanied by his fierce army, the evil Mara approached.
The Lord of Sages defeated him by means of virtues such as generosity.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

Even more fierce than Mara, fighting all night long,
was the obstinate and fierce demon Alavaka.
The Lord of Sages defeated him through the practice of perfect patience.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

The excellent elephant Nalagiri, highly intoxicated,
was as fierce as a forest fire, a raging discus, or a thunderbolt.
The Lord of Sages defeated him by sprinkling the water of loving-kindness.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

With a raised sword in his uplifted hand, the exceedingly fierce
robber Angulimala, running for three leagues;
The Lord of Sages defeated him using the psychic powers of his mind.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

Tying a piece of wood to her belly to appear pregnant,
Cinca made false accusations in the midst of the assembly.
The Lord of Sages defeated her by means of peaceful and gentle conduct.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

Abandoning the truth, Saccaka raised the banner of debate.
With a mind intent on argumentation, he was utterly blinded.
The Lord of Sages defeated him by lighting the lamp of wisdom.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

The Naga king Nandopananda, who was profoundly misguided but possessed great psychic power;
The Lord of Sages had him tamed by his elder son (Moggallana) manifesting as a Naga.
The Lord of Sages defeated him by instructing his disciple in the use of psychic power.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

Whose hand was tightly bitten by the serpent of wrong views,
was the pure, radiant, and powerful Brahma named Baka.
The Lord of Sages defeated him by the medicine of true knowledge.
By that majestic power, may joyous victories be yours.

These eight stanzas of the Buddha's joyous victories,
whoever recites and remembers them diligently day after day,
having overcome many diverse misfortunes,
that wise person will attain the bliss of liberation.
