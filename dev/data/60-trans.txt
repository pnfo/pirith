By the majestic power of the Buddhas established in the ten directions,
may enmities and dangers perish without remainder.
We recite this paritta endowed with such qualities.

The supreme Padumuttara Buddha in the East, and Revata Buddha in the Southeast,
Kassapa Buddha in the South, and Sumangala Buddha in the Southwest,

Sikhi Buddha in the West, and Medhankara Buddha in the Northwest,
Piyadassi Buddha in the North, and Dipankara Buddha in the Northeast,

Kakusandha Buddha on the earth (below), and Saranankara Buddha in the sky (above),
thus, all these Buddhas are established in the ten directions.

May all those sudden dangers, sorrows, diseases, and fears
always perish in all (your) postures.

Whether by yakkhas, deities, kings, thieves, fire, and so forth,
or by all non-humans and animals,
may all arisen adversities perish without remainder.
