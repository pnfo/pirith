The Isigili Discourse

Thus have I heard:
On one occasion
the Blessed One
in Rajagaha
was dwelling
on Mount Isigili.

Then
the Blessed One
the monks
addressed,
saying, "O monks."

"Venerable sir,"
those monks
to the Blessed One
replied.
The Blessed One
said this:

Do you see,
monks,
that Mount Vebhara?
"Yes, venerable sir."
Monks, for that
Mount Vebhara
there was another name,
another designation.

Do you see,
monks,
that Mount Pandava?
"Yes, venerable sir."
Monks, for that
Mount Pandava
there was another name,
another designation.

Do you see,
monks,
that Mount Vepulla?
"Yes, venerable sir."
Monks, for that
Mount Vepulla
there was another name,
another designation.

Do you see,
monks,
that Mount Gijjhakuta?
"Yes, venerable sir."
Monks, for that
Mount Gijjhakuta
there was another name,
another designation.

Do you see,
monks,
this Mount Isigili?
"Yes, venerable sir."
Monks, for this
Mount Isigili
it has always had this very name,
this very designation.

Monks, in the past,
five hundred Paccekabuddhas
on this Mount Isigili
resided for a long time.
As they were entering this mountain,
they could be seen;
but once they had entered, they could not be seen.

Having seen this, people
said thus:
"This mountain
swallows these sages."
Thus the name 'Isigili' (Swallower of Sages) arose.

Monks, I will declare
the names of the Paccekabuddhas.
Monks, I will reveal
the names of the Paccekabuddhas.
Monks, I will teach
the names of the Paccekabuddhas.
Listen to it,
pay close attention,
I will speak."

"Yes, venerable sir,"
those monks
replied to the Blessed One.
The Blessed One said this:

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Arittha
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Uparittha
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Tagarasikhi
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Yasassi
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Sudassana
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Piyadassi
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Gandhara
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Pindola
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Upasabha
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Nita
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Tatha
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Sutava
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili.

Monks, the Paccekabuddha named Bhavitatta
resided for a long time on this Mount Isigili."

Those excellent beings
free from suffering
free from craving
attained full awakening individually;
of those supreme men who have removed the dart of defilements,
listen to me as I proclaim their names.

Arittha,
Uparittha,
Tagarasikhi,
Yasassi,
Sudassana,
and the Buddha Piyadassi,
Gandhara,
Pindola,
and Upasabha,
Nita,
Tatha,
Sutava,
Bhavitatta,

Sumbha,
Subha,
Methula,
and Atthama,
and then Megha,
Anigha,
Sudatha;
these Paccekabuddhas have destroyed the conduit to existence.
Hingu and Hinga,
are of great power.

The two sages named Jali,
and Atthaka,
then the Buddha Kosala,
and then Subahu,
Upanemisa,
Nemisa,
Santacitta,
Sacca,
Tatha,
Viraja,
and Pandita.

Kala and Upakala,
Vijita,
and Jita,
Anga,
and Panga,
and Guttijita.
Passi
abandoned the defilements, the root of suffering.
Aparajita defeated the army of Mara.

Sattha,
Pavatta,
Sarabhanga,
Lomahamsa,
Uccangamaya,
Asita,
Anasava,
Manomaya,
and the destroyer of conceit,
Bandhuma;
Tadadhimutta,
and Vimala,
Ketuma.

and Ketumbaraga,
Matanga,
Ariya,
and then Accuta,
Accutagamabyamaka,
Sumangala,
Dabbila,
Suppatitthita,
Asayha,
and Khemabhirata,
Sorata.

Durannaya,
Sangha,
and also Uccaya,
the other sage Sayha.
of excellent striving,
Ananda, Nanda, Upananda, the twelve,
Bharadvaja
bearing his final body.

Bodhi,
Mahanama,
and also Uttara,
Kesi,
Sikhi,
Sundara,
Bharadvaja.
Tissa and Upatissa,
the cutters of the bonds of existence,
Upasidari,
and Sidari, the destroyer of craving.

There was the Buddha Mangala,
who was free from lust.
Usabha cut the net of craving, the root of suffering.
Upanita attained the tranquil state.
Uposatha,
Sundara,
Saccanama.

Jeta,
Jayanta,
Paduma,
and Uppala,
Padumuttara,
Rakkhita,
and Pabbata,
Manatthaddha,
Sobhita,
Vitaraga,
and the Buddha Kanha,
whose mind was fully liberated.

These and other Paccekabuddhas of great power
have destroyed the conduit to existence.
To those great sages who have transcended all attachments
and attained Parinibbana, you should pay homage, for they are immeasurable.
