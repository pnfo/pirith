The Suriya Paritta (Discourse on the Sun Deity)

Thus
by me
was heard.
On one occasion
the Blessed One
near Savatthi
was dwelling
in the Jeta Grove
of Anathapindika
in the monastery.

Now at that time
the sun deity Suriya
by Rahu, the lord of Asuras,
was seized.

Then
the sun deity Suriya,
the Blessed One
recollecting,
at that time
this stanza
spoke.

Homage
to you,
O Buddha, the Hero!
You are completely freed
everywhere / in every respect.
I have fallen into distress.
To me, who is such,
be a refuge.

Then
the Blessed One,
the sun deity Suriya
referring to,
Rahu, the lord of Asuras
with a stanza
addressed.

To the Tathagata,
the Arahant,
the sun deity Suriya
has gone for refuge.
O Rahu,
release Suriya.
Buddhas
have compassion for the world.

He who
in the pitch-black darkness
creates light,
the shining disc,
of fiery energy;
do not,
O Rahu,
swallow
the moving (sun)
in the sky.
My offspring / my child,
O Rahu,
release
Suriya!', thus He spoke.

Then
Rahu, the lord of Asuras,
the sun deity Suriya
having released,
in great haste,
where
Vepacitti, the lord of Asuras, (was)
there
approached.
Having approached,
anxious,
with hair standing on end (terrified),
on one side
he stood.

To Rahu, the lord of Asuras, who was standing on one side,
Vepacitti, the lord of Asuras,
with a stanza
addressed.

Why
as if in great haste,
O Rahu,
Suriya
did you release?
Having come looking anxious,
why
as if terrified
do you stand?

Into seven pieces
my
would split
head;
even if I were to live,
I would find no happiness.
By the stanzas of the Buddha
I was chanted at / recited to
if I had not released Suriya.
