Of the one endowed with glory, courage, wisdom, and radiant power,
who is an ocean of victorious success, great psychic powers, and mighty virtues,
who possesses an immeasurable accumulation of merit,
who is capable of warding off all dangers,
of the Blessed One, the Worthy One, the Fully Enlightened One—

by the power of the thirty-two marks of a Great Man,
by the power of the eighty minor physical characteristics,
by the power of the one hundred and eight auspicious marks,

by the power of the six-colored aura,
by the power of the halo of light,
by the power of the ten perfections,
by the power of the ten minor perfections,

by the power of the ten ultimate perfections,
by the power of morality, concentration, and wisdom,
by the power of the Buddha, by the power of the Dhamma, and by the power of the Sangha,

by the power of majestic radiance,
by the power of psychic abilities,
by the power of spiritual strengths,
by the power of all knowable phenomena,
by the power of the eighty-four thousand sections of the Dhamma,

by the power of the nine supramundane states,
by the power of the Noble Eightfold Path,
by the power of the eight meditative attainments,
by the power of the six higher knowledges,

by the power of loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity,
by the power of all the perfections,
by the power of taking refuge in the Triple Gem—

may all your sorrows and diseases,
pain, grief, and despair
be completely destroyed!
May all your aspirations be fulfilled!

May you always be endowed with longevity, living for a hundred years!
The skies, mountains, forests, lands, and lakes,
the rivers, and the great ocean,
and their residing guardian deities,
may they always protect you!

By the power of all Buddhas, by the power of all Dhammas, by the power of all Sanghas,
by the power of the three jewels: the jewel of the Buddha, the jewel of the Dhamma, and the jewel of the Sangha,

by the power of the eighty-four thousand sections of the Dhamma,
by the power of the three baskets of the teaching (Tripitaka),
by the power of the disciples of the Conqueror:

May all your diseases, all your fears, all your dangers, all your tribulations, all your bad omens, and all your inauspicious signs be destroyed!

May there always be an increase of lifespan, an increase of wealth, an increase of glory, an increase of fame, an increase of strength, an increase of beauty, and an increase of happiness!

By this majestic power, may all suffering, diseases, fears, enmities, sorrows, tribulations, and numerous dangers be destroyed!

May you have victorious success, wealth, gains, safety, good fortune, happiness, strength, glory, longevity, and beauty! May you have an increase in prosperity and fame, live for a hundred years, and achieve success in your life!
