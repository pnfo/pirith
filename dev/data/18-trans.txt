The Discourse on the Enlightenment Factors to Maha Kassapa

Thus
by me
it was heard.
On one occasion
the Blessed One
in Rajagaha
was dwelling
in the Bamboo Grove
the squirrels' feeding ground.
Now at that time
Venerable Maha Kassapa
in the Pipphali Cave
was dwelling
afflicted,
suffering,
and gravely ill.

Then
the Blessed One,
in the evening
from seclusion
having arisen,
where
Venerable Maha Kassapa was,
there
approached;
having approached,
on the prepared seat
he sat down.
Seated,
the Blessed One
to Venerable Maha Kassapa
said this:

I hope
you
Kassapa,
are bearing up?
I hope
you are keeping going?
I hope
the painful feelings
are decreasing
and not increasing?
Is their decrease apparent,
and not their increase?

Venerable sir, I am not bearing up,
I am not keeping going.
My painful feelings are severe;
they are increasing
and not decreasing.
Their increase
is apparent,
and not their decrease.

Kassapa, these seven factors of enlightenment
by me
are rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
they lead.
What are the seven?

The enlightenment factor of mindfulness
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of investigation of phenomena
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of energy
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of rapture
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of tranquility
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of concentration
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of equanimity
Kassapa,
by me
is rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
it leads.

Kassapa, these seven factors of enlightenment
by me
are rightly expounded,
cultivated
and much developed;
for direct knowledge,
for supreme enlightenment,
for Nibbana
they lead.

Truly,
Blessed One,
they are factors of enlightenment!
Truly,
O Fortunate One,
they are factors of enlightenment!

This the Blessed One said.
The Blessed One
being pleased,
Venerable Maha Kassapa
of the Blessed One
in the words
delighted.

And recovered
Venerable Maha Kassapa
from that illness.
In this way
was abandoned
Venerable Maha Kassapa's
that illness
and entirely ceased.
