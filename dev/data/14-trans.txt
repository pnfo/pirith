The Peacock's Protection

There rises this (sun)
The one with vision
The sole monarch
Golden-colored
Illuminating the earth
Therefore
To you
I pay homage
The golden-colored one
Who illuminates the earth
By you
Today
Guarded
May we dwell
Throughout the day

Those Brahmanas who
Have mastered the ultimate truth
Regarding all phenomena
To them
My
Homage be
And may they
Me
Protect

May there be homage
To the Buddhas
May there be homage
To Enlightenment
Homage be
To the liberated ones
Homage be
To liberation
This (protection)
He (the peacock)
Having made (into his protection)
The peacock
Goes about
Seeking food

I pay homage

By you
Today
Guarded
May we dwell
Through the night

Have mastered the ultimate truth

To them
My
Homage be
And may they
Me
Protect

To the Buddhas
May there be homage
