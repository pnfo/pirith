Homage to the thirty-seven factors of awakening:
the four foundations of mindfulness, the four bases of spiritual power,
the four right efforts, the four noble truths,
the five faculties, the five powers, the seven factors of awakening,
the noble eightfold path.
The mantra of the Buddha is a mantra, the mantra of the Dhamma is a mantra, the mantra of the Sangha is a mantra.
May all diseases, all afflictions and all dangers be destroyed!
