The Discourse on Downfall

Thus have I heard:
On one occasion
the Blessed One
was dwelling in Sāvatthi,
at Jeta's Grove,
in Anāthapiṇḍika's monastery.

Then,
a certain deity,
when the night was far advanced,
of stunning beauty,
the entire Jeta's Grove,
having illuminated,

where the Blessed One was,
approached there.
Having approached,
and respectfully greeted the Blessed One,
he stood to one side.

Standing to one side,
that deity
to the Blessed One,
spoke in verse:

About the declining man,
we ask Gotama;
having come to ask the Blessed One:
what is the cause of downfall?

The one who progresses is easily known;
the one who declines is easily known.
The lover of Dhamma progresses;
the hater of Dhamma declines.

Thus we understand this;
this is the first downfall.
Tell us the second, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

The wicked are dear to him;
he does not hold the virtuous dear;
he delights in the teachings of the wicked—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the second downfall.
Tell us the third, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

Being fond of sleep,
fond of socializing,
a person who lacks exertion,
who is lazy,
and prone to anger—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the third downfall.
Tell us the fourth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

Whoever, mother or father,
who have grown old and past their youth,
does not support despite having the means to do so—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the fourth downfall.
Tell us the fifth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

Whoever, a brahmin or an ascetic,
or any other mendicant,
deceives by telling lies—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the fifth downfall.
Tell us the sixth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

A person with abundant wealth,
with gold and food,
who enjoys delicious food all alone—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the sixth downfall.
Tell us the seventh, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

Proud of one's birth, proud of one's wealth,
and proud of one's clan, the person
who looks down upon their own relatives—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the seventh downfall.
Tell us the eighth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

A debauchee in women, a drunkard,
and a gambler, the person
who squanders whatever is gained—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the eighth downfall.
Tell us the ninth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

Not contented with one's own wives,
he is seen with prostitutes,
and is seen with the wives of others—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the ninth downfall.
Tell us the tenth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

A man past his youth,
marries a young girl with breasts like timbaru fruits;
he cannot sleep due to jealousy of her—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the tenth downfall.
Tell us the eleventh, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

A woman who is addicted to drink and squanders wealth,
or a man of a similar kind,
whom one places in a position of authority—
that is a cause of downfall.

Thus we understand this;
this is the eleventh downfall.
Tell us the twelfth, O Blessed One;
what is the cause of downfall?

With little wealth but great craving,
a person born in a noble (warrior) family,
who aspires to kingship in this world—
that is a cause of downfall.

Having observed these causes of downfall in the world,
the wise person, observing them thoroughly,
the noble one, endowed with insight,
partakes in the realm of ultimate bliss (Nirvana).
