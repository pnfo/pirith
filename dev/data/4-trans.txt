What is the one?
All beings
subsist on food.

What is the two?
Name and form.

What is the three?
The three feelings.

What is the four?
The four Noble Truths.

What is the five?
The five aggregates of clinging.

What is the six?
The six internal sense bases.

What is the seven?
The seven factors of enlightenment.

What is the eight?
The Noble Eightfold Path.

What is the nine?
The nine abodes of beings.

What is the ten?
With ten factors
endowed,
"an Arahant,"
one is called.
