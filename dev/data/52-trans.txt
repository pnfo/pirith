The Discourse on the Quail

Fulfilling the perfections for awakening,
having been born in the form of a quail,
by whose majestic power the forest fire
avoided the Great Being.

To the Elder Sāriputta,
it was spoken by the Lord of the World.
Having great power lasting for an aeon,
that protection, we shall now recite.

There is in this world the virtue of morality,
truthfulness, purity, and compassion.
By the power of that truth, I shall make
an unsurpassed asseveration of truth.

Having reflected upon the power of the Dhamma,
and remembering the former Conquerors,
relying on the power of truth,
I made this asseveration of truth:

There are wings, but they cannot fly;
there are feet, but they cannot walk;
Mother and father have departed.
O fire, retreat!

As soon as this truth was made by me,
the great blazing fire
avoided sixteen tracts of land,
just as a fire goes out upon reaching water.

There is nothing equal to my truth.
This is my perfection of truth.
The Discourse on the Quail is concluded.
