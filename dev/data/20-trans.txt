The Discourse on the Factors of Enlightenment to Maha Cunda.

Thus
by me
it was heard.
At one time
the Blessed One
in Rajagaha
was dwelling
in the Bamboo Grove,
at the Squirrels' Sanctuary.
Now at that time
the Blessed One
was afflicted with a disease,
suffering,
and gravely ill.

Then
Venerable Maha Cunda,
in the evening,
from solitary retreat
having arisen,
where
the Blessed One was,
there
he approached.
Having approached
the Blessed One,
having paid homage,
to one side
he sat down.

To the one seated to one side,
Venerable Maha Cunda,
the Blessed One
said this:
"May they become clear
to you,
Cunda,
the factors of enlightenment."

"Venerable Sir, these seven factors of enlightenment
by the Blessed One
have been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
they lead.
What are the seven?

The mindfulness factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

The investigation of Dhamma factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

The energy factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

The rapture factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

The tranquility factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

The concentration factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

The equanimity factor of enlightenment,
Venerable Sir,
by the Blessed One
has been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
it leads.

Venerable Sir, these seven factors of enlightenment
by the Blessed One
have been well expounded,
cultivated,
and frequently practiced;
for higher knowledge,
for perfect enlightenment,
and for Nibbana
they lead."

"Indeed,
Cunda,
they are factors of enlightenment.
Indeed,
Cunda,
they are factors of enlightenment."

Thus spoke
Venerable Maha Cunda.
The Teacher approved.

And arose
the Blessed One
from that illness.
Thus
was eradicated
for the Blessed One
that illness,
and it completely ceased to be.
