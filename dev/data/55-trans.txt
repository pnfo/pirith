The greatly compassionate Lord,
for the welfare of all living beings,
having fulfilled all the perfections,
attained the supreme enlightenment.
By the uttering of this truth,
may there be a joyous victory for you!

Just as the Buddha was victorious at the root of the Bodhi tree,
enhancing the delight of the Sakyans;
similarly, may there be victory for you!
May you achieve joyous victory!

Having revered the jewel of the Buddha,
the supreme and excellent medicine,
which brings welfare to gods and humans:
By the safe power of the Buddha,
may all adversities be destroyed!
May all your sufferings be appeased!

Having revered the jewel of the Dhamma,
the supreme and excellent medicine,
which cools the fevers of defilements:
By the safe power of the Dhamma,
may all adversities be destroyed!
May all your fears be appeased!

Having revered the jewel of the Sangha,
the supreme and excellent medicine,
worthy of offerings and hospitality:
By the safe power of the Sangha,
may all adversities be destroyed!
May all your illnesses be appeased!

Whatever jewel exists in the world,
diverse and numerous,
there is no jewel equal to the Buddha.
Therefore, may there be safety for you!

Whatever jewel exists in the world,
diverse and numerous,
there is no jewel equal to the Dhamma.

Whatever jewel exists in the world,

there is no jewel equal to the Sangha.

There is no other refuge for me,
the Buddha is my supreme refuge.
By the uttering of this truth,
may there be a joyous victory for you!

There is no other refuge for me,
the Dhamma is my supreme refuge.
By the uttering of this truth,

There is no other refuge for me,

May all misfortunes be avoided,
may all illnesses be destroyed,
may no dangers come to you,
may you have happiness and long life.

May there be every blessing,
may all deities protect you.
By the power of all the Buddhas,
may you always be safe and well!

May there be every blessing,
may all deities protect you.
By the power of all the Dhammas,
may you always be safe and well!

May there be every blessing,
may all deities protect you.
By the power of all the Sanghas,
may you always be safe and well!

Of planets, stars, yakkhas, and spirits,
warding off the evil planetary influences,
by the power of this protective chanting (Paritta),
may their associated afflictions be destroyed!

Whatever bad omens and inauspicious signs,
whatever unpleasant sounds of birds,
evil planetary influences, and unpleasing nightmares:
may they be brought to destruction by the power of the Buddha!

Whatever bad omens and inauspicious signs,
whatever unpleasant sounds of birds,
evil planetary influences, and unpleasing nightmares:
may they be brought to destruction by the power of the Dhamma!

Whatever bad omens and inauspicious signs,
whatever unpleasant sounds of birds,
evil planetary influences, and unpleasing nightmares:
may they be brought to destruction by the power of the Sangha!

May those who have fallen into suffering be free from suffering,
may those who have fallen into fear be free from fear,
may those who have fallen into sorrow be free from sorrow!
May this be so for all living beings.

May beings give charity with faith,
may they observe moral conduct at all times,
may they delight in meditation,
and may the deities who have gathered here return safely.

and by the majesty of the Arahants,
I bind this protection in every way.
