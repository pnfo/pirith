He who will be supreme, called the Buddha of the twenty-four,
is the supreme Jalanandana, endowed with the power of the perfections.

The water at the bank of the Anoma river, the supreme bowl and robes:
that water of the perfections cuts all bonds.

The great elder named Ananda, the supreme treasurer of the Dhamma:
that by which the bond upon the great elder was destroyed.

Thus, by that power of the Dhamma of the glorious Buddhas of the world,
may charms and spells be removed and destroyed by the power of the Buddha.

He is the Lord of sages, homage to the Buddhas, who crushes the army of Mara.
Ten thousand crores: the cutting of all bonds.

He has the virtues of the perfections; he will be supreme,
through the round of many births, a thousandfold, by the power of the Dhamma.

Lying down, or the thousands, the person of supreme virtue:
by the eighty and by all of them, may every success be yours!
