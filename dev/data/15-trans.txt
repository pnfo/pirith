The Discourse on the Moon Deity

Thus
by me
heard.
On one occasion
the Blessed One
near Savatthi
was dwelling
in Jeta's Grove,
of Anathapindika
in the monastery.

Now at that time
the moon deity Chanda
by Rahu, the lord of the Asuras,
was seized.

Then
the moon deity Chanda,
the Blessed One
recollecting,
at that time
this stanza
spoke:

Homage
to you,
O Buddha, the Hero!
You are completely freed
in every respect.
I have fallen into distress;
to me, who is such,
be a refuge.

Then
the Blessed One
the moon deity Chanda
referring to,
to Rahu, the lord of Asuras
with a stanza
addressed:

To the Tathagata,
the Arahant,
Chanda
has gone for refuge.
O Rahu,
release Chanda.
The Buddhas
are compassionate to the world.

Then
Rahu, the lord of Asuras,
the moon deity Chanda
having released
in great haste
where
Vepacitti, the lord of Asuras, [was]
there
approached.
Having approached,
agitated
with hair standing on end,
to one side
stood.

To Rahu, the lord of Asuras, who was standing to one side,
Vepacitti, the lord of Asuras,
with a stanza
spoke:

Why
like one in great haste,
O Rahu,
Chanda
did you release?
Having come looking agitated,
why
like a terrified person
do you stand?

Into seven pieces
my
would split
head;
while living
I would find no happiness.
By the verses of the Buddha
I was chanted upon;
if I had not released Chanda.
