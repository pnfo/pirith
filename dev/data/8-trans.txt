The Great Discourse on Blessings

Thus
by me
was heard
On one occasion
the Blessed One

in Savatthi
was dwelling
in Jeta's Grove
of Anathapindika
in the monastery

Then
a certain deity
when the night was far advanced

of surpassing radiance
the entire Jeta's Grove
illuminating

where the Blessed One was
there approached

Having approached
the Blessed One
having respectfully greeted
to one side
stood

At one side
standing, that deity
to the Blessed One
in verse
spoke

Many deities
and humans
upon blessings
have pondered

desiring
well-being
please declare
the supreme blessings.

Not associating
with fools
and associating with the wise

honoring those worthy of honor
this
is a supreme blessing.

Residing in a suitable place
having done meritorious deeds in the past

and setting oneself on the right course
this
is a supreme blessing.

Vast learning
and proficiency in crafts
a highly trained discipline

and well-spoken words
this
is a supreme blessing.

Supporting one's mother and father
cherishing spouse and children

and peaceful occupations
this
is a supreme blessing.

Generosity
and righteous conduct
to relatives
and helping

blameless actions
this
is a supreme blessing.

Ceasing and abstaining from evil
and refraining from intoxicants

and diligence
in wholesome practices
this
is a supreme blessing.

Reverence
and humility
and contentment
gratitude

listening to the Dhamma at the right time
this
is a supreme blessing.

Patience
amenability to correction
and seeing ascetics

discussing the Dhamma at the right time
this
is a supreme blessing.

Austerity
and the holy life
seeing the Noble Truths

and the realization of Nibbana
this
is a supreme blessing.

touched
by worldly conditions
the mind
whose
does not tremble

sorrowless
stainless
secure
this
is a supreme blessing.

Such things as these
having fulfilled
being unconquered everywhere

everywhere
go safely
that
for them
is the supreme blessing.
