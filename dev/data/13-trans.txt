The Discourse on the Benefits of Friendship

Having abundant food
he becomes
when journeying away
from his own home;
many people
relying on him
live off him.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Whatever country he goes to,
to a town
or to a capital city,
everywhere
he is honored.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Not to him
do thieves
overpower;
nor does despise him
the royalty;
all enemies
he overcomes.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Unangered
to his own home
he returns;
in the assembly
he is joyfully welcomed;
among relatives
he becomes supreme.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Having honored others,
he is honored;
he is revered,
being respectful to others;
he bears praise and fame.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

One who honors (others)
receives honor;
one who venerates
receives veneration in return;
status
and fame
he attains.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Like a fire
he blazes forth;
like a deity
he shines;
by prosperity
he is unforsaken.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Cows
for him
breed;
in the field
what is sown
sprouts and grows;
he enjoys the fruit of his children.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

From a cleft
or from a mountain
or from a tree
fallen,
a person
(when) dropped,
finds support.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray,

Having deep and firmly grown roots,
like a banyan tree (unmoved) by the wind,
enemies
cannot overpower him.
Whoever
to friends
does not betray.
