The Mahamoggallana Bojjhanga Paritta (The Discourse on the Factors of Enlightenment to Mahamoggallana)

Thus
by me
it was heard.
On one occasion
the Blessed One
in Rajagaha
was dwelling
in the Bamboo Grove (Veluvana),
at the Squirrels' Sanctuary.
Now at that time,
Venerable Maha Moggallana
on Mount Vulture Peak (Gijjhakuta)
was dwelling,
being afflicted,
suffering,
and gravely ill.

Then,
the Blessed One,
in the evening,
from seclusion
having arisen,
where
Venerable Maha Moggallana was,
there
approached.
Having approached,
on the prepared seat
he sat down.
Having sat down,
the Blessed One
to Venerable Maha Moggallana
said this:

I hope
you,
Moggallana,
are bearing up?
I hope
you are getting better?
I hope
your painful feelings
are decreasing
and not increasing?
Is their decrease apparent,
and not their increase?

Venerable sir, I am not bearing up.
I am not getting better.
My painful feelings are severe,
they are increasing
and not decreasing.
Their increase
is apparent,
not their decrease.

Moggallana, these seven factors of enlightenment,
by me
are rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
they lead.
What are the seven?

The enlightenment factor of mindfulness,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of investigation of phenomena,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of energy,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of rapture,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of tranquility,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of concentration,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

The enlightenment factor of equanimity,
Moggallana,
by me
is rightly expounded;
when cultivated
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
it leads.

Moggallana, these seven factors of enlightenment,
by me
are rightly expounded;
when cultivated,
and frequently practiced,
to direct knowledge,
to full enlightenment,
and to Nibbana,
they lead.

Truly,
O Blessed One,
these are factors of enlightenment!
Truly,
O Sublime One,
these are factors of enlightenment!

This was said
by the Blessed One.
Glad at heart,
Venerable Maha Moggallana
in the Blessed One's
words
delighted.

recovered
And Venerable Maha Moggallana
from that illness;
in such a way
was abandoned
Venerable Maha Moggallana's
that illness
entirely.
