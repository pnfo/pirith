The Discourse on the Advantages of Loving-Kindness

Thus
by me
was heard.
On one occasion
the Blessed One
in Sāvatthī
was dwelling
in Jeta's Grove,
Anāthapiṇḍika's
monastery.

Thereupon
the Blessed One
the monks
addressed,
saying, 'O monks.'
'Venerable Sir,'
those monks
to the Blessed One
replied.
The Blessed One
said this:

Of loving-kindness,
O monks,
of the liberation of mind,
when cultivated,
developed,
practiced frequently,
made into a vehicle,
made into a foundation,
established,
made familiar,
and properly undertaken,
eleven benefits
are to be expected.
What eleven?

1. One sleeps happily;
2. One wakes happily;
3. One sees no evil dreams;
4. One is dear to humans;
5. One is dear to non-humans;

6. Deities protect one;
7. Neither to him
does fire,
or poison,
or weapons
cause harm;
8. Quickly
the mind
becomes concentrated;
9. The facial complexion becomes serene;
10. Unconfused,
one passes away;
11. If penetrating no higher,
one is reborn in the Brahma world.

Of loving-kindness,
O monks,
of the liberation of mind,
when cultivated,
developed,
practiced frequently,
made into a vehicle,
made into a foundation,
established,
made familiar,
and properly undertaken,
these eleven benefits
are to be expected.

Said this
the Blessed One.
Delighted,
those monks
in the Blessed One's
words
rejoiced.
