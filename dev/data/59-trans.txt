The brave conquerors who ascended the seat of victory,
Having defeated Mara together with his army,
Those Lords of Men who drank the immortal nectar of the Four Noble Truths,
The Buddhas, beginning with Tanhankara,
The twenty-eight leaders,
All those Lords of Sages are established on (your) head.

The Buddhas are established on the head,
And the Dhamma
In (your) eyes,
The Sangha is established for (you)
In the chest, the mine of all virtues.

And Anuruddha in the heart,
And Sariputta on the right,
Kondanna at the back,
Moggallana on the left.

In (your) right ear
Are Ananda and Rahula,
Kassapa and Mahanama,
Are both in the left ear.

At the ends of the hair, at the back,
Radiant like the sun,
Sits the glorious one,
The great sage Sobhita.

The one named Kumara Kassapa,
The great sage, the brilliant speaker,
He is constantly in (your) mouth,
Established, the mine of virtues.

Punna and Angulimala,
Upali, Nanda, and Sivali,
These five elders became,
Tilaka marks on (your) forehead.

The remaining eighty great elders,
The victorious disciples of the Conqueror,
Shining with the majesty of virtue,
Are established on your various limbs.

The Ratana Sutta is in front,
The Metta Sutta on the right,
The Dhajagga Sutta is behind,
And the Angulimala Sutta on the left.

The Khandha and Mora Parittas,
And the Atanatiya Sutta,
Are a canopy in the sky,
And the rest are established as a rampart.

Endowed with the power of the Conqueror's authority,
Adorned by the rampart of the Dhamma,
For you dwelling in the four postures,
Always in the cage of the Fully Enlightened One,
Born of wind, bile, and so forth,
Outer and inner afflictions,
May they all be completely destroyed by the majestic power of infinite virtues.

Standing in the midst of the Conqueror's cage,
Dwelling on the earth,
May they all protect you always,
Those great majestic beings.

Thus being extremely well protected,
With dangers overcome by the Conqueror's power,
With the host of enemies destroyed by the Buddha's power,
Walk, protected by the power of the True Dhamma.

Thus being extremely well protected,
With dangers overcome by the Conqueror's power,
With the host of enemies destroyed by the Dhamma's power,
Walk, protected by the power of the True Dhamma.

Thus being extremely well protected,
With dangers overcome by the Conqueror's power,
With the host of enemies destroyed by the Sangha's power,
Walk, protected by the power of the True Dhamma.

You are encircled by the rampart of the True Dhamma.
The eight Noble Ones are in the eight directions.
In between them are the eight Protectors (Buddhas).
The Conquerors stand above (you) like a canopy.

Destroying Mara's army,
Standing on (your) head,
The Teacher, having ascended the Bodhi seat,
Moggallana dwells on the left arm, Sariputta on the right.
The Dhamma dwells in the middle of the chest for freedom from existence. To the peacock womb,
The Bodhisatta reached. At the pair of feet is the Sun, the sole protector of the world.

All inauspiciousness, dangers, and bad omens,
All calamities, diseases, planetary faults, and boundless blame,
All obstacles, fears, and unpleasant bad dreams,
May they go to destruction by the supreme power of the Buddha.

All inauspiciousness, dangers, and bad omens,
All calamities, diseases, planetary faults, and boundless blame,
All obstacles, fears, and unpleasant bad dreams,
May they go to destruction by the supreme power of the Dhamma.

All inauspiciousness, dangers, and bad omens,
All calamities, diseases, planetary faults, and boundless blame,
All obstacles, fears, and unpleasant bad dreams,
May they go to destruction by the supreme power of the Sangha.
