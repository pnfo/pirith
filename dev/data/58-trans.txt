To the life of the Buddha,
no one is capable of causing any danger.

To the six-hued rays of the Buddha,
no one is capable of causing any danger.

To the thirty-two marks of a Great Man of the Buddha,
no one is capable of causing any danger.

To the four requisites offered to the Buddha,
no one is capable of causing any danger.

To these four marvelous attributes,
no one is capable of causing any danger.

By the utterance of this truth, may all dangers completely fade away,
and may Mara and his forces be destroyed! May victorious success be yours!
