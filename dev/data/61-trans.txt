The Discourse on the Factors of Enlightenment

For those wandering in the cycle of rebirth,
destroying all suffering,
the seven factors of enlightenment,
and crushing the army of Mara;

Whichever beings, having understood these (factors),
the supreme ones freed from the three worlds of existence,
to the birthless, ageless, disease-free,
and fearless state of the deathless (Nirvana) have gone.

Endowed with such and other qualities,
a collection of manifold virtues,
this medicine and this mantra,
we now recite this Bojjhanga (Paritta).

The enlightenment factor of mindfulness,
likewise the investigation of phenomena,
energy, rapture, and tranquility,
are the subsequent factors of enlightenment.

The enlightenment factors of concentration and equanimity,
these seven, by the All-Seeing
Sage, have been perfectly expounded,
cultivated and frequently practiced.

They lead to profound knowledge,
to realization (of Nirvana) and to enlightenment.
By the speaking of this truth,
may you always be well!

On one occasion, the Protector (the Buddha),
seeing Venerable Moggallana and Kassapa,
sick and suffering,
preached the seven factors of enlightenment.

And they, rejoicing in that (discourse),
were freed from the illness at that very moment.
By the speaking of this truth,
may you always be well!

Once, when the King of Dhamma (the Buddha) himself
was afflicted by sickness,
He requested Venerable Cunda
to recite it (the Paritta) with devotion.

Rejoicing (in the recitation), from that illness
He recovered instantly.
By the speaking of this truth,
may you always be well!

Those illnesses were eliminated
for those three great sages,
like defilements destroyed by the Path,
attaining the state of non-arising.
By the speaking of this truth,
may you always be well!
